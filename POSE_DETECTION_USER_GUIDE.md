# 🤖 AI Pose Detection User Guide

Welcome to the AI-powered pose detection feature! This guide will help you get the most out of your workouts with intelligent form tracking and rep counting.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### What is Pose Detection?

Our AI pose detection uses your device's camera and advanced machine learning to:

- **Automatically count your reps** - No more manual counting!
- **Analyze your form in real-time** - Get instant feedback on your technique
- **Track your progress** - See how your form improves over time
- **Provide personalized coaching** - Get specific tips for better performance

### Supported Exercises

Currently supported exercises with AI pose detection:

- 💪 **Push-ups** - Perfect for upper body strength
- 🦵 **Squats** - Great for lower body power
- ⏱️ **Planks** - Core stability and endurance
- 🏋️ **Sit-ups** - Abdominal strength training
- 🔥 **Burpees** - Full-body cardio workout

## 📱 Setup and Calibration

### First Time Setup

1. **Grant Camera Permission** - The app needs camera access to track your movements
2. **Find Good Lighting** - Ensure you have adequate, even lighting
3. **Clear Your Space** - Remove clutter from your workout area
4. **Position Your Device** - Follow the camera positioning guide below

### Camera Positioning Standards

#### 📐 General Guidelines

- **Distance**: Position your device 6-8 feet away from your workout area
- **Height**: Place camera at chest height when you're standing
- **Angle**: Ensure your entire body is visible in the frame
- **Background**: Use a clear, uncluttered background
- **Lighting**: Avoid backlighting; face a light source when possible

#### 🎯 Exercise-Specific Positioning

**Push-ups & Planks**

- **Side Profile View** (90° angle to your body)
- Camera should capture your full body from head to feet
- Start with your face visible before beginning the exercise

**Squats**

- **45° Diagonal Front View**
- Position camera to see both knees and shoulders clearly
- Ensure hip movement is visible throughout the range of motion

**Sit-ups**

- **Side Profile View** (90° angle to your body)
- Camera should capture your torso and legs fully
- Start in lying position with face visible

**Burpees**

- **45° Diagonal Side View**
- Wider frame needed to capture multi-phase movements
- Position camera 8-10 feet away for full movement range

### 🔧 Calibration Process

1. **Tap "Calibrate"** when starting a new exercise
2. **Stand in Position** - Arms at your sides, facing the camera angle for your exercise
3. **Wait for Confirmation** - The app will analyze your position and lighting
4. **Adjust if Needed** - Follow on-screen guidance for optimal positioning

## 🎮 Using Pose Detection

### Starting a Workout

1. **Select Your Exercise** from the workout menu
2. **Toggle AI Detection** - Switch on the AI toggle in the top-right corner
3. **Complete Calibration** if prompted
4. **Tap Start** when you're ready to begin

### During Your Workout

#### 📊 What You'll See

- **Skeleton Overlay** - Shows detected body landmarks (can be toggled off)
- **Rep Counter** - Large, easy-to-read count with progress ring
- **Form Feedback** - Real-time tips and corrections
- **Form Score** - Percentage showing your technique quality

#### 🎯 Form Feedback System

The AI provides feedback in order of importance:

1. **🚨 Safety Issues** (Red) - Stop and correct immediately
2. **⚠️ Form Corrections** (Orange) - Important technique adjustments
3. **✅ Rep Validation** (Yellow) - Rep counting status
4. **💪 Encouragement** (Green) - Motivational messages

#### 📈 Performance Indicators

- **FPS Counter** - Shows camera processing speed
- **Form Score** - Real-time percentage (aim for 80%+)
- **Streak Counter** - Consecutive good-form reps

### Manual Fallback

If pose detection isn't working optimally:

1. **Toggle off AI Detection** to switch to manual mode
2. **Tap +1** after each rep to count manually
3. **Re-enable AI** when conditions improve

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### 📷 Camera Issues

**"Camera not working"**

- Check that no other apps are using the camera
- Restart the app
- Restart your device if needed

**"Poor detection quality"**

- Improve lighting conditions
- Clean your camera lens
- Move to a less cluttered background
- Adjust your distance from the camera

### 🤖 AI Detection Problems

**"Reps not counting"**

- Ensure you're performing the full range of motion
- Check that your entire body is visible
- Slow down your movements for better detection
- Verify you're in the correct camera position for your exercise

**"Inconsistent feedback"**

- Maintain steady lighting throughout your workout
- Keep the same camera position
- Avoid wearing loose or baggy clothing
- Ensure your workout space remains clear

**"Low form scores"**

- Review the exercise technique guides
- Start with slower, more controlled movements
- Focus on one correction at a time
- Consider lowering the difficulty level temporarily

### ⚡ Performance Issues

**"Slow or laggy detection"**

- Close other apps to free up memory
- Enable Performance Mode in settings
- Reduce the render mode to "Minimal" or "Markers-only"
- Lower the target frame rate

**"App crashes during workouts"**

- Restart the app
- Check available device storage
- Update to the latest app version
- Contact support if issues persist
